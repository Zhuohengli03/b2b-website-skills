Process & numbers

From Wire to Sorted Part, In-House

DIN912-M6x20-12.9-BO 04 / 04
  1. 01
    Cold heading

    Multi-station cold headers form head and shank from wire in one pass, no material cut away.

  2. 02
    Thread rolling

    Threads are rolled, not cut, to tolerance 6g; the unbroken grain flow gives higher fatigue strength.

  3. 03
    Heat treatment In-house

    Our mesh-belt furnace hardens and tempers to class 10.9 and 12.9. No outside heat treater.

  4. 04
    100% optical sorting

    Every automotive part passes camera sorting for dimensional and surface defects before packing.
