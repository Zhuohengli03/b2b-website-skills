**Standards & grades**
- **Q1 ISO 4762 or DIN 912 — which should I specify?**（实测 56 词 / 上限 80）：Both numbers describe the same hexagon socket head cap screw. DIN 912 is withdrawn and ISO 4762 is the current standard, so German distributors list the ISO number first; the dimensions you receive are identical. We mark every size with both numbers and accept either on your purchase order. Fine-pitch threads are covered by ISO 12474.
- **Q2 What is the difference between class 12.9 and 10.9?**（实测 74 词 / 上限 80）：Both are property classes to ISO 898-1; the first digit gives the nominal tensile strength, the second the yield-to-tensile ratio, so 12.9 is the stronger and harder of the two. Choose 12.9 where clamp load per bolt matters most, typically socket head cap screws in machine building; choose 10.9 for automotive flange bolts, where the lower hardness gives more margin against hydrogen embrittlement. Our DIN 912 M6 × 20 in 12.9 tests at ≥ 1,220 MPa.
- **Q3 Black oxide, zinc plated or zinc flake?**（实测 67 词 / 上限 80）：Black oxide is a thin conversion coating with minimal corrosion protection, usual for socket head cap screws used indoors with oil. Zinc plating is electrolytic and gives moderate protection. Zinc flake coating (Dacromet-type) is applied without electrolysis, so it suits class 10.9 and above where hydrogen embrittlement must be avoided; our DIN 6921 10.9 flange bolts reach ≥ 720 h salt spray. Zinc plating on our parts `[待确认：是否提供，D20]`.
